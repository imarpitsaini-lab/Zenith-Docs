Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zenith collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use the application. Your privacy is extremely important to us, and Zenith has been designed with a privacy-first approach.

1. About Zenith

Zenith is a digital wellbeing and productivity application developed by Arpit Saini. Its primary purpose is to help users build healthier digital habits by allowing them to monitor app usage, set usage limits, block distracting applications, and improve focus through Deep Focus sessions.

Zenith performs almost all processing locally on your device. Your personal usage information is not uploaded to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

Zenith may access or process the following information:

  • Application usage statistics (only after you grant Usage Access permission).
  • Protected application selections that you manually configure.
  • Daily usage limits created by you.
  • Focus session information such as duration, completion status and goals.
  • Analytics generated from your own usage data.
  • Application preferences stored locally on your device.

Zenith does NOT collect your passwords, messages, photos, videos, contacts, emails, banking information or personal files.

3. Permissions Used

Accessibility Service

Zenith uses Android Accessibility Service only after you explicitly enable it. This permission allows Zenith to detect when a protected application is opened, display the blocking screen, manage daily time limits, and operate Deep Focus sessions.

Zenith does not use Accessibility Service to read personal conversations, capture passwords, record keystrokes, or monitor sensitive personal content.

Usage Access Permission

Zenith requires Usage Access permission to calculate application usage time, daily statistics, analytics, and productivity reports.

Without this permission, Zenith cannot measure how long applications have been used.

Display Over Other Apps

Zenith requests overlay permission in order to display its blocking screen and floating timer while enforcing your selected limits or active Focus Sessions.

This permission is used solely for Zenith's core functionality.

Notifications

Zenith may display notifications related to Focus Sessions, daily reminders, or application updates.

Notifications never contain sensitive personal information.
